Your fleet at a glance

See multiple servers together in an expanded dashboard. Disk usage, CPU and memory, upload and download traffic, and unread notification counts sit side by side, helping you compare resource usage and spot servers that need attention.

Server monitoring in your pocket

Keep key server metrics within reach in a compact layout. Clear usage bars and gauges show storage, CPU and memory consumption, while the network panel displays upload and download rates. The navigation keeps Home, Notifications, Actions and Settings close at hand.

Keep track of what needs attention

Review server events in one notification view, with descriptions, timestamps and severity levels from informational updates to critical issues. Group and sort notifications to organise your view, and use the notification controls to manage events such as low disk space, elevated memory usage, backup results and expiring certificates.
